Built for people with long watchlists

Everything is optional. Turn on what you want, ignore the rest.

Episode-level tracking

Mark episodes as you go, rate what you finish, and pick straight back up where you left off.

Discover by genre or season

Browse the whole AniList catalogue — what's trending, what's airing now, or anything from fantasy to slice of life.

XP, levels and achievements

Every episode earns experience. Level up your profile and unlock achievements as your history grows.

Collections that make sense to you

Group series however you like — comfort shows, rewatches, "someday" — instead of one endless backlog.

Alerts when episodes drop

A live airing schedule for everything you're watching, with notifications you can tune or switch off entirely.

Bring your list with you

Import everything you've already logged on AniList or MyAnimeList. No starting from scratch.

No account. No ads. No data collected.

Use guest mode and everything stays on your device — Apple lists WatchMori as collecting no data at all. Sign in with Apple only if you want your list synced across devices, and social features stay off until you turn them on.

  • Guest mode by default
  • No third-party analytics
  • Export or delete anytime
